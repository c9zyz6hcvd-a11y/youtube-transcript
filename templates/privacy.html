Privacy Policy 私隱政策

Last updated: June 2026

What we collect 我哋收集啲咩

  • No accounts. You don't sign up, so we hold no profile about you. 唔使登記，我哋冇你嘅個人檔案。
  • Transcripts are processed on the fly. The video link and transcript you submit are processed in memory to serve your request and are not stored on our servers. 逐字稿即時處理，唔會儲存。
  • History stays in your browser. The "Recent" list is saved in your own browser's localStorage only. You can clear it anytime. 「Recent」紀錄只存喺你部機。
  • Basic technical logs (such as IP address) may be kept temporarily for rate-limiting and abuse prevention. 只為防濫用而短暫保留基本技術紀錄。

Third-party services 第三方服務

  • YouTube — video title and thumbnail are fetched from YouTube's public oEmbed endpoint.
  • AI processing — transcripts you choose to process with AI are sent to a third-party AI API (DeepSeek) to generate the cleanup or summary, then discarded by us.
  • Advertising — if ads are shown on this site, they are served by Google AdSense, which may use cookies to personalise ads. See Google's advertising policy. You can opt out of personalised ads in your Google settings.

Cookies

The tool itself sets no tracking cookies. Advertising partners (if enabled) may set cookies as described above. 工具本身唔落 tracking cookie；廣告夥伴（如有）或會按上述方式使用 cookie。

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via the GitHub repository for this project. 有疑問可經本項目嘅 GitHub 聯絡。
