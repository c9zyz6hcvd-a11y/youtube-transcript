About this tool 關於呢個工具

This free tool extracts the transcript (subtitles/captions) of any YouTube video, then uses AI to clean it up into a readable script or summarize it — in English, 繁體中文（香港／台灣）, 簡體中文, 日本語 or 한국어. Great for podcasts, lectures, interviews and long-form videos you don't have time to watch.

免費擷取任何 YouTube 影片嘅字幕逐字稿，再用 AI 整理成易讀文章或者摘要（支援港式廣東話書面語）。啱晒聽 podcast、長片、講座 — 唔使睇足全程都攞到重點。

How to use 點用

  1. Paste a YouTube link and press Transcribe — 貼上 YouTube 連結，撳 Transcribe
  2. Pick a language, then choose ✦ AI 整理 (full cleanup), ⚡ 快速摘要 (quick summary) or 📋 詳細摘要 (detailed notes)
  3. Copy the result, or export as PDF / Word / .txt — 一鍵匯出

FAQ 常見問題

Is it free? 免費㗎？

Yes — extracting transcripts is unlimited. AI cleanup/summaries are free with a daily limit per user. 擷取字幕無限制；AI 功能每人每日有合理用量上限。

Which videos work? 咩片用得？

Any video with captions, manual or auto-generated. If the uploader disabled captions, there is no transcript to extract. 影片要有字幕（人手或自動生成）先用得。

Do I need an account? Is my data stored? 要唔要登記？會唔會儲起我嘅嘢？

No account needed. Transcripts are processed on the fly and not stored on our servers — your history lives only in your own browser. 唔使登記；逐字稿即時處理，唔會儲喺伺服器，「Recent」紀錄只存喺你自己部機。

What's special about the Hong Kong Chinese output? 港式中文有咩特別？

The AI rewrites transcripts in natural Hong Kong-style written Cantonese (廣東話書面語) — not stiff Mandarin-style 書面語. 出嚟係自然港式行文，唔係硬譯普通話腔。

Why "No transcript found"? 點解話冇字幕？

The video has captions disabled, or YouTube hasn't generated them yet. Try another video. 影片關咗字幕功能，或者 YouTube 未生成字幕。
